The problem

Finding what a procedure requires shouldn’t mean knowing someone

Scattered

Official information is spread across ministry sites, Facebook pages and Telegram channels.

Unsearchable

Much Khmer guidance is posted as images, often undated and without an owner.

Paid for

People lose a day with the wrong documents, or pay a broker for free information.

SuperAsk: ask in your own words, get the answer with its official source attached, or an honest “we have no verified source.”

Honest refusals

When it won’t answer, the trace says why

ReasonWhat it means
policyNot allowed: emergencies, legal advice, personal cases, politics
scopeNot a government-service question at all
retrievalNo approved source covers it, so it’s a content gap
verificationA written answer was withheld for an unsupported claim
moderationThe finished answer failed the output check

No hand-off: SuperAsk says “No approved source covers this” and stops.

SuperAsk.AI · Demo 1 · 11

Unit testing · One test up close

Catching an invented fee before a citizen sees it

// tests/engine/verify.test.ts

it("catches an invented fee", () => {

  const result = verify(

    "The fee is 45,000 riel.",

    ["X-1"],

    [source("X-1", "A fee of 30,000 riel

        applies on renewal.")],

    ["X-1"],

  );

  expect(result.passed).toBe(false);

  expect(result.unsupported).toContain("45000");

});

Arrange

The answer says 45,000 riel; the source says 30,000

Act

Run the verification gate on the answer

Assert

It fails, and names 45000 as unsupported
