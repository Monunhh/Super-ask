The problem

Finding what a procedure requires shouldn’t mean knowing someone

Scattered

Official information is spread across ministry sites, Facebook pages and Telegram channels.

Unsearchable

Much Khmer guidance is posted as images, often undated and without an owner.

Paid for

People lose a day with the wrong documents, or pay a broker for free information.

SuperAsk: ask in your own words, get the answer with its official source attached, or an honest “we have no verified source.”

YEAN: Government information in Cambodia is scattered across many sites and channels, a lot of it is posted as images, and people end up paying brokers for information the state publishes for free. SuperAsk lets anyone ask in their own words and get an answer with the official source attached. When we have no verified source, it says so instead of guessing.
