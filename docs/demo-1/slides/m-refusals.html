ReasonWhat it means

MONUNHH: A refusal is not one thing. Policy means we are not permitted to answer, such as emergencies or legal advice. Scope means it is not a service question. Retrieval means we have no approved source, which is a content gap for the data team. Verification means the model wrote something the sources do not support, so we withheld it.
