Leave this slide up while switching to the browser. Every question below was tested on the live site on 9 Nov 2026. Type them exactly; do NOT use the landing-page topic buttons except “Driving licence renewal” in English and “Birth certificate”. PHALLAMONUNHH (UC-1, UC-2): Open superask.vercel.app/chat. Type “How do I renew my driving licence?”. Point out: the answer lists documents (old licence, ID card, fitness certificate, three 4x6 photos), the 30,000 riel fee and the service centres, all quoted from the Ministry of Public Works and Transport page that retrieval found (UC-2). Then switch to Khmer and type: តើការបន្តប័ណ្ណបើកបរត្រូវការឯកសារអ្វីខ្លះ? The reply comes back in Khmer from the same page. Then ask the follow-up “How much does it cost?”: it keeps the topic and answers 30,000 riel. Business goals: bilingual access and comprehensive information. HEEJAE (UC-3): On the same answer, open the source card. Show the ministry name in English and Khmer, the document, the legal instrument (Prakas No. 047, 27 January 2017), the effective date, the last-verified date and the link to the official page. The badge says “Past review date”: that is the system working. The sample source was last verified 31 July 2026 and its 90-day review is due, so SuperAsk tells the citizen to confirm with the ministry rather than hiding it. Business goal: trustworthy answers. SEREYPROPHEA (UC-4): New chat. Type “What is the weather in Phnom Penh tomorrow?”. SuperAsk declines, says it only answers government-service procedures, and suggests what it can do. Business goal: trustworthy answers. Do not use passport, business registration or land title questions: they are not in the knowledge base yet and retrieval returns an unrelated passage (known issue, being fixed for Demo 2). BUNKHEANG (UC-7): On a phone or Telegram Desktop, open @superask168_bot and send “Where do I apply for a driving licence?”. The reply lists the service centres (AEON Mall 1 and 2, the one-window units, the provincial departments), the free hotline 1275, info@mpwt.gov.kh and office hours, with the source. Then open the Mini App button to show the same chat inside Telegram. Test Telegram before the slot. Business goal: ministry contact and location. DOUNGKHE (UC-11): In a terminal at the project folder, run: npx tsx scripts/crawl.ts --mode=monitor --only=SRC-021 --dry-run. It takes about 30 seconds: talk while it runs. It checks 15 pages of moc.gov.kh and reports pages fetched and changes found; dry run means nothing is saved. Explain: we monitor 132 official sites; a change goes to a human reviewer and never becomes an answer on its own. Business goal: comprehensive, current information. Avoid the transport ministry site (SRC-034): it was too slow to answer during testing.
