For Cambodian citizens

who need the steps, documents, fees and office for a government service. Today that information is scattered across ministry sites, Facebook and Telegram, often as undated images, so people lose a day or pay a broker.

What is new: unlike a general chatbot, it never invents a fee. Every answer carries its official source and review date.

Five use cases, four business goals

Use case

Business goal

What you will see

UC-1 Ask · UC-2 Retrieve

Bilingual access
Comprehensive information

“How do I renew my driving licence?” in English, then in Khmer: the answer quotes the ministry page in the language asked. Follow-up: “How much does it cost?”

UC-3 View citation

Trustworthy answers

The source card: ministry, Prakas No. 047, effective and last-verified dates, review status, link to the official page.

UC-4 Decline

Trustworthy answers

“What is the weather in Phnom Penh tomorrow?” It declines and says what it does cover, instead of guessing.

UC-7 Office contact

Ministry contact and location

In Telegram: “Where do I apply for a driving licence?” Service centres, hotline 1275, email, office hours.

UC-11 Refresh content

Comprehensive, current information

The monitor checks moc.gov.kh page by page. Changes go to a reviewer, never straight into answers.
