Who it is for · what it is

Finding out what a procedure requires shouldn’t mean knowing someone

For Cambodian citizens

who need the steps, documents, fees and office for a government service: a driving licence, a birth certificate, a vehicle, a trademark, a bank complaint.

Today that information is scattered across ministry sites, Facebook and Telegram, often as undated images in Khmer, so people lose a day or pay a broker.

What is new about SuperAsk

Source first. It answers only from approved ministry content, and every answer carries its source and review date.

Declines instead of guessing. A general chatbot invents a fee; SuperAsk refuses when no source supports the answer.

Khmer and English. Ask in either language; the reply follows yours.

One engine, three doors. Web chat, Telegram bot and Telegram Mini App.
