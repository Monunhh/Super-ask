YEAN: SuperAsk is for Cambodian citizens who need to know how a government procedure works: what documents to bring, what it costs, and which office handles it. Today that information is scattered across ministry websites, Facebook pages and Telegram channels, often posted as images, so people lose a day with the wrong documents or pay a broker. What is new: SuperAsk answers only from approved official content and shows the source and its review date with every answer. When there is no source, it says so instead of guessing, which general chatbots do not do. It works in Khmer and English, and the same engine serves the web chat, a Telegram bot and a Telegram Mini App.
