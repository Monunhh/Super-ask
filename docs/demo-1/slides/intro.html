SuperAsk.AI · Demo 1 · 2

PHALLAMONUNHH: SuperAsk is for Cambodian citizens who need to know how a government procedure works: what to bring, what it costs, which office handles it. Today that information is scattered across ministry websites, Facebook pages and Telegram channels, so people lose a day or pay a broker. Here is how SuperAsk works, left to right: you ask in Khmer or English, on the web or in Telegram. It searches only approved ministry content, checks that what it found really supports an answer, and then either answers with the official source attached, or declines and tells you what it does cover. It never invents a fee, which is the difference from a general chatbot. On the right is what it can answer today: 45 approved passages across five services from four ministries, with vehicle registration and driving licences the largest.
