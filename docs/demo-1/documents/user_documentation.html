1. About SuperAsk.AI

SuperAsk.AI answers questions about Cambodian government procedures: what documents you need, what it costs, how long it takes and which office to go to. You can ask in Khmer or English. Every answer shows the official source it came from, so you can check it yourself. When SuperAsk has no source that supports an answer, it says so instead of guessing.

Important. SuperAsk.AI is a student prototype, not a government office. Its content is a sample built from public ministry pages and has not been formally approved by the ministries. Always check the linked official source before acting on a fee, deadline or requirement.

2. What you need

  • A phone, tablet or computer with an internet connection and a recent web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox), or the Telegram app.
  • Nothing to install and no account. The login and register screens on the site are prototypes and are not needed to use the chat.

3. Ask a question on the website

  1. Open https://superask.vercel.app in your browser.
  2. Choose Ask on the web, or go straight to https://superask.vercel.app/chat.
  3. Type one question about a government service in the message box, for example: How do I renew my driving licence?
  4. Press Enter or the send button. The answer appears in a few seconds.
  5. Read the answer, then look at the source card under it (see section 5).
  6. Ask a follow-up in the same conversation, for example: How much does it cost? SuperAsk remembers which procedure you were asking about.

Tip: name the specific service. "Birth certificate for a newborn" gets a better answer than "documents for my baby".

4. Ask in Khmer or English

Ask in either language and SuperAsk replies in the language you used. To switch the language of the buttons and menus, use the Khmer / English switch at the top of the page. Khmer questions work with or without spaces between words.

5. Read the answer and its source

PartWhat it tells you
AnswerThe steps, documents, fee, processing time or office for your question, taken from the official source
Source cardThe ministry, the document (for example a Prakas number), the date it took effect, the date it was last checked, its review status, and a link
Source linkOpens the official ministry page so you can confirm the answer

If the dates look old or the answer seems incomplete, open the link and confirm with the ministry before you go.

6. When SuperAsk declines

SuperAsk declines rather than guesses. You will see a decline when:

  • the question is not about a government service (for example, What is the weather tomorrow?);
  • it asks for legal advice, a decision on your personal case, or a prediction;
  • it is an emergency (SuperAsk gives emergency numbers instead);
  • no approved source covers the question yet.

The decline tells you what SuperAsk does cover, so you can rephrase or go to the right office.

7. Find the office, phone number and hours

Ask where to go, for example Where do I apply for a driving licence? or, after asking about a procedure, Can I have the contact and location of the office for this? SuperAsk shows the service centre, the hotline (for driving licences, 1275), email and office hours when the official source lists them. If no contact details are published, it tells you so and gives the office location instead.

8. Use the Telegram bot and Mini App

  1. Open Telegram and search for @superask168_bot.
  2. Open the chat and press Start.
  3. Type your question as a normal message and send it.
  4. The reply includes the answer and its source, in the language you asked in.
  5. For the full chat screen inside Telegram, press the SuperAsk button to open the Mini App.

The bot, the Mini App and the website use the same answer engine, so they give the same answer to the same question. If a Telegram follow-up is misunderstood, repeat the name of the procedure.

9. Conversations, feedback and privacy

  • New conversation: use the New chat button to start another topic.
  • Earlier conversations: open them from the sidebar (on a phone, open the menu first). They are stored in your browser only.
  • Delete: remove one conversation or clear all history from the sidebar.
  • Feedback: answer Was this helpful? Yes / No under an answer, or choose Report to flag a wrong answer. The team reviews these reports.
  • Privacy: do not type ID card numbers, phone numbers, passwords or bank details. SuperAsk removes such numbers from its logs, but it never needs them.

10. Common problems

ProblemWhat to try
SuperAsk declines my questionName the exact service and, if you know it, the ministry. The topic may not be covered yet (see section 11).
The answer seems out of dateOpen the source link and check the dates; confirm with the ministry.
The Telegram bot does not replyCheck your connection, make sure you opened @superask168_bot, or use the website.
Wrong languageUse the Khmer / English switch, and ask the whole question in one language.
A follow-up is misunderstoodRepeat the procedure name, especially in Telegram.
The page does not loadRefresh, or try again later; the prototype host may be briefly unavailable.

11. What SuperAsk covers today

The prototype covers five services: driving licences, vehicle registration, civil registration (such as birth certificates), trademarks, and consumer-finance complaints to the National Bank. It answers from 45 official source passages and 5 approved answers, and explains 14 official terms. Other services will be declined for now. Passports, business registration, real user accounts and an office directory with map links are planned for the final demo.
